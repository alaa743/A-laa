Portrait scrambler

Photo: 864 × 1056, 9 × 11 tiles of 96 px. Each tile is stored in a 112 px cell with an 8 px gutter of its real neighbouring pixels, so JPEG colour bleed between shuffled cells lands in the gutter (discarded on reassembly) instead of showing as seams. Cells are multiples of 16 px to match JPEG blocks.
